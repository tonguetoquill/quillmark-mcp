# Bundled fonts — all OFL 1.1

- **Manufacturing Consent** (Regular) — NYT-masthead revival of Chomsky. https://github.com/googlefonts/manufacturing-consent-font
- **Playfair Display** (Variable + Italic Variable) — display serif for headlines. https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/playfairdisplay
- **Source Serif 4** (Variable + Italic Variable) — Matthew-Carter-lineage news serif for body. https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif
- **Libre Franklin** (Variable + Italic Variable) — Franklin Gothic revival for bylines and metadata. https://github.com/impallari/Libre-Franklin

All fonts are SIL Open Font License 1.1.
